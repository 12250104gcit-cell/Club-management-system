Student app (phone UI)

1. Copy the contents of src/ over your project's src/ folder
   (App.jsx, main.jsx, components/, data/, pages/, styles/).
2. Copy public/logo.png into your project's public/ folder.
3. Keep your existing club photos in public/ (integrity.jpg, literary.jpg, mmd.jpg,
   rovers.jpg, startup.jpg, tarayana.jpg).
   Optional: public/integrity-logo.png (owl logo for the Home card) and
   public/profile.jpg (student photo). Both fall back automatically if missing.
4. npm run dev
